Study Grove for Mac
===================

1. Copy the whole StudyGrove folder onto the Mac (Desktop is fine).
2. Right-click StudyGrove.app, choose Open, then click Open.
   A normal double-click is often blocked until you do this once.
3. If macOS still says it cannot be opened, open Terminal and run:

   xattr -cr ~/Desktop/StudyGrove/StudyGrove.app

   then right-click StudyGrove.app → Open again.
4. Allow the microphone when Voice Chat asks.
5. In Settings, add an OpenAI key so Voice Chat can talk.
